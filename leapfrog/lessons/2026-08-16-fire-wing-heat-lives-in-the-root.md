# 2026-08-16 — Fire wing: heat lives in the ROOT, and the trailing edge is the coldest tissue

**Did / learned.** Wing Lab stream F1 researched what makes a wing read as a *fire* dragon's
wing beyond being orange, and wrote `reforged/wing-lab/data/F1-fire-wing.md`. The load-bearing
finding is anatomical and it inverts the default: infrared thermography of a bat in level flight
measures **34 °C at the forearm muscle mass and under 24 °C at the trailing edge, with "the
majority of the wing 1–2 °C above ambient"**. A wing is a *radiator*, and a radiator is mostly
cold. Every cheap fire dragon puts its brightest line on the trailing edge — which in life is the
coldest tissue on the animal. Three more sourced inversions came with it: the heat sits in
**discrete windows with an "abrupt skip of temperature"** at their border (elephant pinna), not in
a soft gradient; the radiator is **proximal and VENTRAL**, i.e. on the face a rear-chase camera
mostly cannot see; and recruitment is **root-first, tip-last** with the radiator fully OFF below a
threshold (toucan bill). The gotcha for shading: a hot *semi-transparent* membrane obeys
ε = 1 − exp(−κd/cos θ), so it is brightest **edge-on** — the exact inverse of §7.4's
`dot(N,V)^k` seam dial. Seams flare facing you; membranes flare turning away.

**→ Systematize.** The generalisable move is **"look up where the heat physically is before
deciding where the glow goes."** Any emissive creature part has a real thermal analogue with
published thermography — wings (bats), ears (elephants), bills (toucans), fins, crests. The
pattern that keeps falling out is the same three-part one, and it should be the default template
for every future emissive appendage: **one hard-edged hot WINDOW at the attachment root · a few
terminating vessel LINES running outward and dying before mid-span · 93–97 % of the surface
completely dark.** Two reusable tests come with it: (a) an emissive-area probe — cruise 3–6 % of
the part's projected area, ≤15 % ever, ≤1 % clipped white — and (b) a "is the glow on the hidden
face?" check, because putting the emitter on the anatomically correct ventral/inner surface gives
withheld glow for free instead of as a stylistic choice. The canon audit backs the low numbers
hard: Balerion's "scales and wings were black", Ancalagon is *the Black*, and Rathalos — the
literal Fire Wyvern — puts **black** ornament on the membrane and the red on the plating.

**→ Leapfrog.** Two things get cheap now. First, **a sun behind the dragon lights the wing for
free in the fire palette**: blood-perfused tissue transmits in the 650–950 nm optical window, so
backlit transmission and thermal emission land in the *same* hue family and can be authored as one
continuous ladder instead of two fighting systems — the richest zero-cost win in the wing. Second,
**the wing now has three incommensurate rhythms** (vessel vasomotion 0.33–0.67 Hz, flap ~1.2 Hz,
buoyant plume flicker f = 1.75/√D ≈ 2.3 Hz) that share no period, which is a general cure for the
metronome cheap-tell in `AAA-PIPELINE.md` — and the vessel pulse *flattens* under exertion, so
"calm throbs, hard flight goes steady" is a sourced motion tell nobody else will have.
