## SCRATCH — confirmed numbers so far (will be folded into Tables)

- Bat wing membrane thickness **0.039–0.267 mm** (39–267 µm) [S] Swartz/academia + RG
- Bat skin **4–10× thinner** than predicted for body size [S]
- Elastic modulus **~3 MPa spanwise → ~30 MPa chordwise** (≈10:1 anisotropy) [S] cites Swartz 1996
- Modulus nonlinear: **<0.1 MPa at low strain**, order-of-magnitude rise at realistic strain [S]
- Plagiopatagium = weakest + most extensible region; uropatagium = greatest load at failure [S]
- Plagiopatagium length change **>50%** spanwise AND chordwise in flight [S]
- Q. lawsoni: humerus **23–25 cm**, radius/ulna **36–39 cm**, mcIV **46–47 cm** [S] Padian 2021
